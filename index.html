About Royal Designing Multi Centre

Royal Designing Multi Centre is a graphic design and printing business located at 13 WFPArea, Magburaka, Northern Province, Sierra Leone.

We specialise in creative graphic design, T-shirt printing, business cards, posters, flyers, school badges, wedding invitations, sign writing, signpost production and stamp cutting.
